Rules

RULE 01 How to eliminate someone?

You eliminate your target by pulling their flag off.

How do I know my target?

You will be given an envelope with your name and your number on it. The number on the envelope is YOUR NUMBER .

Inside the envelope, you will also find two cards:

WHITE CARD
ID Card

Your number

BLACK CARD
Target Card

Your target's number

RULE 02 What to Do After an Elimination?

1. Take your target's flag

After eliminating someone, take the flag you removed from your target.

2. Drop it off at your House Base

Put the flag into the designated box in your House Base room:

GREEN HOUSE
Room 2405
YELLOW HOUSE
Room 2401
RED HOUSE
Room 2412
BLUE HOUSE
Room 2414

3. Earn points for your House!

Collected flags count towards your House's points and contribute to the House competition.

Important

The flag's colour does not matter. Put the flag of the person you eliminated into your own House's box.

RULE 03 What to Do After You Pull a Flag

1. Check that you eliminated the correct target

Check the ID number on the person's card against the target number in your envelope to make sure you eliminated the correct person.

2. Clip the card and put it in the box

Clip the card and place it in the box outside Ms. Chen's room .

3. Wait for the board to be updated

Ms. Chen or Mrs. Yuan will check the cards in the box and remove your target's name from the board. Only Ms. Chen or Mrs. Yuan may remove photos from the Ninja Tag Photo Wall.

You must wait until your target's name has been removed from the board before going after your target's target.

RULE 04 Automatic Elimination & Game Time

Automatic Elimination

Absent for 2 Days

If you are absent for 2 days, including EA, you are automatically eliminated.

Not sure if your target has been eliminated due to absence? It is YOUR responsibility to secretly ask Ms. Yuan to check.

No Flag = Automatic Elimination

If you have no flag, you are automatically eliminated.

Target Updates & Game Time

Target Number Updates

Target numbers are updated at: 8:00, 9:00, 10:00, 11:00 AM, 12:00, 1:00, 2:00, 3:00, and 3:30 PM.

Game Time: 9:40 AM – 3:40 PM

Tagging is allowed only during game time. No tagging before 9:40 AM or after 3:40 PM.

RULE 05 Activity Areas & Off-Limits Zones

Where Can You Play?

4th-Floor Hallway

Allowed during game time.

5th-Floor Area

Allowed during lunch time only.

Outdoor Campus

Allowed during break time only. All courts are off-limits.

Where Is Playing Prohibited?

You must not tag or play in any of these areas:

Restricted Areas

Classrooms, library, gym, offices, copy room, meeting rooms, staircases, bathrooms, and cafeteria.

Restricted Buildings & Floors

Chinese Track Building and the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd floors of ET.

Be respectful. Safety always comes first!

RULE 06 Do's and Don'ts

Important Rules to Follow

1. Keep Your Target Secret

Do NOT tell anyone who your target is.

2. Stay Calm and Respectful

Do not get angry or violent. Ninja Tag is just a fun game. Always treat others with respect.

3. Follow All Rules

Do not break or ignore any game rules.

4. No Help from Friends

Do not ask friends to help you tag or surround your target. You must play on your own.

5. Stay Out of Prohibited Areas

Do not eliminate anyone in a prohibited area. Breaking this rule will get you eliminated from the game.

6. Never Touch the Photo Wall

Do not remove or touch any photos on the Ninja Tag Photo Wall. Find Ms. Chen or Ms. Yuan if a photo needs to be removed.

7. Only Tag Your Assigned Target

Do not eliminate anyone who is not your assigned target. It wastes time and does not help you progress in the game.

Play fair, respect others, and have fun!
